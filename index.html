Any bank, any cheque: line it up once

Every cheque book and every printer is a little different. That is why the calibrator is the heart of CheQPrinter. Measure your cheque, adjust until each printed field sits exactly where it belongs, and save it. From then on it is remembered.

  • Enter your cheque's size in millimetres, or upload a scan of it to line up against
  • Drag and resize every field on the cheque, or type exact positions
  • Save a separate alignment profile for each printer
  • Fine-tune the print position in 0.5 mm steps and choose where the cheque sits on the A4 page
  • Print a ruler-and-crosshair test sheet on plain paper before using a real leaf
  • Save a template per bank, then export and share it with colleagues

Built-in layouts, including named starting points for many UAE banks, are only starting points. Your calibrated template is what makes it exact.

⚠

Always test before using real cheque leaves

Print the test sheet and a test cheque on plain paper first, then hold it against a real cheque. Banks set their own rules on printed cheques, so confirm with your bank. CheQPrinter is not affiliated with any bank.

From download to first cheque in four steps

Set-up takes a few minutes the first time. After that, printing a cheque takes seconds.

  1. Download and open

    Save the single HTML file, then open it with Chrome or Edge (right-click → Open with). Bookmark it for next time.

  2. Start from a layout

    Pick a built-in layout, then measure your own cheque with a ruler and enter its width and height.

  3. Calibrate once

    Print the test sheet on plain A4, hold it against a real cheque, and drag the fields until they line up. It is saved per bank.

  4. Fill in and print

    Enter date, payee and amount. Words are written for you. Check the preview, then print on your cheque.

Your data stays on your computer

There is no account and no cloud. Templates, beneficiaries and history are stored inside your browser on this computer. That keeps your information private, and it also means you are responsible for backing it up.

  • Use the same browser and open the same file location each time
  • Clearing browser data erases your cheque data
  • Download a backup file regularly and keep a copy off this PC
  • Backups made in Lite can be restored in Plus
💾

Updating or moving to a new PC?

  1. In your current version, open Backup / Restore and download a backup.
  2. Download the new version and open it in the same browser.
  3. Open Backup / Restore, choose Merge, and select your backup file.

Frequently asked questions

Is CheQPrinter really free?
Yes. Lite and Plus are both free. CheQPrinter is a passion project and there are no plans to sell it.
Does it work with my bank's cheque?
Yes. The calibrator lets you measure your own cheque and position every printed field exactly where it belongs, so it is not limited to any list of banks. Built-in layouts, including named starting points for many UAE banks, are only starting points: calibrate once with the test sheet and the app remembers it. Bank names are used for identification only.
Can I use it outside the UAE?
Yes. The calibrator works with any cheque, anywhere. At first start you choose your country and default currency. AED, SAR, USD, EUR, GBP, PKR and INR are pinned at the top, dozens more are included, and decimals follow the currency. Can't find yours? Add a custom currency with your own symbol or code. Amount in words is written in English or Arabic, and you can always type your own words in any language using Edit Words.
Does it work on Mac, or only Windows?
It is a single HTML file that runs in a modern browser, so it works on Windows, macOS and Linux. Chrome or Edge are recommended for printing.
Is my cheque data uploaded anywhere?
No. There is no account and cheque data is not sent to any server. Everything is stored in your browser on your computer, which is why regular backups matter.
Do I need an internet connection?
CheQPrinter is an offline-first app: no account, no cloud, and your cheque data is never sent anywhere. Right now the page styling and icons are fetched from the internet when the file opens, so do the first open (and any update) while online. A fully self-contained build is on our list.
Which printers work?
CheQPrinter is made for ordinary A4 desktop printers (inkjet or laser). A cheque is a small, stiff leaf, so use the manual or bypass slot if your printer has one, and always test on plain paper first. Large photocopiers and A3 machines are not recommended: in our own test, a cheque got stuck in the rollers of a big copier. Results depend on your printer's paper feed, which is exactly what the alignment tools are for.
Will my bank accept a printed cheque?
Banks set their own rules on printed cheques, ink and layout. Check with your bank, and test on plain paper before using a real leaf.
What is the difference between Lite and Plus?
Lite prints cheques. Plus adds multiple companies and bank accounts, a cheque register, batch cheques, installment schedules, a PDC dashboard, cheque book tracking, vouchers and remittance advice.
