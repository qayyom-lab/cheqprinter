Tools to prepare and print cheques

Set up your cheque layout, calibrate your printer and check the result on plain paper.

Calibrate your cheque layout

Use millimetre rulers to position fields. Boxes show the real text, visually centred, with a crosshair at the left of the text baseline. Arrow keys move a field by 0.1 mm; hold Shift to move 1 mm. Built-in layouts are starting points.

A profile for every printer

Save the alignment for each printer you use and switch in one click: paper feed, tray and fine-tune offsets in 0.5 mm steps.

Test sheet before real leaves

Print on plain paper. Red crosses mark the same text-start point used in calibration; compare the sheet with your cheque before using a real leaf.

Currency settings

Set your country and default currency in Currencies & defaults. Choose built-in or custom currencies. Arabic amount words are off by default; automatic Arabic detection applies only to a new install.

Offline app, local data

After download, the app works offline and makes no network calls. Cheque data is stored in your browser on this computer. The website uses Google Analytics for website visits and downloads.

Print and backup controls

Review the preview, respond to duplicate warnings, and create backups. Keep a backup outside the browser so you can restore it if needed.

Calibrate a layout for your cheque

Cheque books and printers can differ. Use the millimetre rulers and field preview to adjust your layout, then save it. Print a test sheet on plain paper and compare it with your cheque before using a real leaf.

  • Enter your cheque size in millimetres or upload a scan already cropped to the cheque edges; scans fit the frame by default
  • Position each field using the rulers; the selected field crosshair marks the left of the text baseline. Arrow keys move 0.1 mm; Shift + arrow moves 1 mm
  • Save a separate alignment profile for each printer and switch between them in one click
  • Fine-tune the print position in 0.5 mm steps and choose where the cheque sits on the A4 page
  • Print a test sheet with red crosses at the same text-start point as the calibration crosshair
  • Save a template per bank, then export and share it with colleagues

More than 80 built-in layouts, including starting points for many UAE banks, are available to help you begin. Check your alignment with a test sheet; results depend on your printer and cheque.

From download to first cheque in four steps

Set-up takes a few minutes the first time. After that, printing a cheque takes seconds. Read the full step-by-step guide with screenshots and video.

  1. Download and open

    Save the single HTML file, then open it with Chrome or Edge (right-click → Open with). No internet is needed after that. Bookmark it for next time.

  2. Start from a layout

    Pick a built-in layout, then measure your own cheque with a ruler and enter its width and height.

  3. Calibrate and test

    Use the rulers and field crosshair, then print the test sheet on plain A4. Compare it with your cheque and adjust as needed. Save the template and a printer profile.

  4. Fill in and print

    Enter date, payee and amount. Words are written for you. Check the preview, then print on your cheque.

Your data stays on your computer

There is no account and no cloud. Templates, beneficiaries and history are stored inside your browser on this computer; cheque data is not uploaded. Plus offers an optional PIN lock and auto-lock to help prevent casual access. The PIN does not encrypt saved data or backup files, so protect access to your computer and backups. You are responsible for keeping regular backups. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and downloads; the apps themselves send nothing to anyone.

  • Works fully offline: no internet is needed once the file is downloaded
  • Use the same browser and open the same file location each time
  • Clearing browser data erases your cheque data
  • Download a backup file regularly and keep a copy off this PC
  • Backup / Restore shows how much browser storage you have used, and the app warns you before it fills up
  • Lite and Plus backups are compatible for shared data in both directions
  • The Last Backup link opens Backup & Restore and blinks when a backup is overdue
  • Merge adds backup data; Overwrite replaces current data and offers a safety copy first
  • Data Reset downloads a safety backup and requires you to type RESET
  • The Plus PIN lock does not encrypt saved data or backup files
💾

Updating or moving to a new PC?

  1. In your current app, open Backup / Restore and download a backup.
  2. Download the updated app file and open it in the same browser.
  3. Open Backup / Restore, select your file, and choose Merge to add data or Overwrite to replace current data. A safety copy is offered before Overwrite.

Frequently asked questions

Is CheQ Printer really free?
Yes. Lite and Plus are both free, and we plan to keep them that way. CheQ Printer is a passion project. In the future we may offer a separate advanced edition, for example a desktop version, for a fee; it would be optional and would not replace or limit these free editions.
Does it work with my bank's cheque?
The calibrator lets you adjust field positions for your cheque. Built-in layouts, including named layouts for many UAE banks, are starting points. Print a test sheet and check with your bank before using a real cheque. Bank names are used for identification only.
Can I use it outside the UAE?
Yes. The calibrator lets you adjust the layout for your cheque. In Lite open Currencies & defaults (in Plus: Settings, then Currency list) to choose your country and default currency. AED, SAR, USD, EUR, GBP, PKR and INR are pinned at the top, dozens more are included, and decimals follow the currency. Can't find yours? Add a custom currency with your own symbol or code. Amount in words is written in English or Arabic amount words are off by default. You can also type your own words in any language using Edit Words.
Does it work on Mac, or only Windows?
It is a single HTML file that runs in a modern browser, so it works on Windows, macOS and Linux. Chrome or Edge are recommended for printing.
Is my cheque data uploaded anywhere?
No. There is no account and cheque data is not sent to any server. Everything is stored in your browser on your computer, which is why regular backups matter.
Does the Plus PIN lock encrypt my data?
No. The optional PIN lock helps prevent casual use of the app interface, but it does not encrypt saved data or backup files. Protect access to your computer and keep backups secure.
Do I need an internet connection?
Only to download the file. After that, Lite and Plus work fully offline: all styling, icons and fonts are inside the file, and the apps make no network calls and cheque data stays in your browser. You can open it with the internet disconnected.
Can I save settings for more than one printer?
Yes. In Printer Setup, give the alignment a name (for example "Office HP LaserJet") and save it as a profile. Add one for every printer you use and load any of them in one click. Profiles are included in your backup.
How many cheques does Lite remember?
There is no fixed limit. Print history is kept in your browser, which varies by browser and device; cheque records are tiny, so that is many thousands of entries. Backup / Restore shows how much space you have used and the app warns you before it fills up. Scanned cheque images take the most space.
How do I know my download is genuine?
Both downloads have a SHA-256 checksum listed under the Lite and Plus cards on this page (tap "SHA-256" to open it). On Windows run certutil -hashfile cheqprinter-lite.html SHA256; on Mac or Linux run shasum -a 256 cheqprinter-lite.html. If the result matches the published value, the file is exactly the one we published.
Which printers work?
CheQ Printer is made for ordinary A4 desktop printers (inkjet or laser). A cheque is a small, stiff leaf, so use the manual or bypass slot if your printer has one, and always test on plain paper first. Save each printer as its own alignment profile. Large photocopiers and A3 machines are not recommended: in our own test, a cheque got stuck in the rollers of a big copier. Results depend on your printer's paper feed, which is exactly what the alignment tools are for.
Will my bank accept a printed cheque?
Banks set their own rules on printed cheques, ink and layout. Check with your bank, and test on plain paper before using a real leaf.
What is the difference between Lite and Plus?
Lite provides cheque writing, printing, calibration, printer profiles, beneficiary management, unlimited print history and backup / restore, and works fully offline. Plus does all of that and adds multiple companies and bank accounts, a full register and activity history, batch and installment schedules, a PDC maturity dashboard, cheque book and leaf controls, voucher and invoice tracking, print confirmations, signature or seal stamps, and optional PIN lock with auto-lock.
